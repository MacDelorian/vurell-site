What you get

Two parts, sold together. The template is the system. The course is how you make it run on your market.

  1. 01Private GitHub repository

    The template

    A company OS for Claude Code, with the go-to-market parts built in

    • A constitution for Claude (CLAUDE.md), agents with roles, and every repeatable job written as a skill with its own success criteria and run log.
    • Signal sourcing: careers pages, EU public tenders, accelerator directories, each company scored.
    • Qualification: an evidence pack per company, and a qualifier calibrated blind against your own past decisions before it touches a new row.
    • Contacts: an email-finding waterfall across free tiers, and the domain setup that keeps mail out of spam.
    • Messages and replies: short-message rules, a signal-led sequence, and reply scoring in 11 buckets.
    • Lead magnets built from public data, rendered to PDF.

Who it is for

Founders and go-to-market people at software companies who use Claude Code, or want to, and would rather own their outbound system than rent it.

  • No LinkedIn bot

    The template never logs in to LinkedIn or sends there. The course covers a pace you keep by hand.

  • No bought lists of people

    Companies come from public signals. People are looked up one company at a time, for a reason you can name.

  • No promised numbers

    You get the system and the method. Replies and calls depend on your market and your offer.

  • No code background needed, but a terminal

    You run commands and edit text files. Claude Code writes most of the rest; the course shows how.

Questions

What do I need to run it?
Claude Code on a paid Claude plan, Python 3.10 or newer, and Git. Data tools are optional; the template starts on their free tiers.
Is the template mine to keep?
Yes. You get access to the private repository and keep your own copy of it.
What if I can't make the live sessions?
Sessions run on Thursdays at 18:00 Paris time (12:00 in New York) on Google Meet, and every one is recorded. Office hours run on Mondays at 12:00 Paris time.
Can I get a refund?
Yes, in full, until the second session.
Is this the same as hiring Vurell?
No. Vurell runs outbound for you. GTM OS is for teams that want to run it themselves.
