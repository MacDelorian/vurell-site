What the pilot includes

  1. 01Week one

    Signal and list

    Agencies call it: ICP, lead lists, intent data

    • The public signal that says your buyer needs what you sell now, picked with you for your product.
    • The companies showing that signal, each with the evidence and a link to it.
    • The exclusions: who looks right and is not, and why.
    • The person to write to at each company.
  2. 02Week one

    Message

    Agencies call it: copywriting, cold email

    • A four-step email sequence in your name. Each first email names the signal the company shows.
    • LinkedIn notes from your own account, if you want them.
    • Every claim in your messages checked against something you can prove.
    • You read every message before the first send.
  3. 03The whole month

    Sending and booking

    Agencies call it: outbound, appointment setting, SDR as a service

    • Two secondary domains you own, about €20, set up with their domain records and a warm-up.
    • Sending in your name. You hold every account and every password.
    • Follow-up calls by phone after the emails, in English, French or Ukrainian.
    • Replies handled, and the first call with each interested company run by us.
    • The ones that are ready handed to your team with notes: the need, the timing, who decides.
  4. 04Every week

    Report

    Agencies call it: pipeline reporting

    • A weekly report: sent, replies, calls booked, calls held, and the reason for every no.
    • One change tested at a time, written down before it is read.
    • At the end you keep the company list, the messages and every reply.

What counts as a call

Before the pilot starts we agree in writing what a qualified lead is: the company size, the country, and the role of the person we speak to.

Only a first call we held, with a company that fits that definition, handed to you with notes, is billed, at $300. A call that does not happen costs nothing.

What we do not do

  • Promise that a company will buy.

    We run the first call and hand you the ones that are ready. You sell, and nothing about the outcome of a call is guaranteed.

  • Send without your read.

    You see every message before the first send.

  • Lists by the thousand.

    Every company on the list shows the signal, with the evidence and a link.

  • Long retainers.

    The pilot is 30 days. After it you decide whether to continue.

Questions

How soon do calls start?
The first week goes to the signal, the list, the messages and the domains. New domains need a warm-up, so sending starts small and grows over the month.
What do I need to provide?
What you sell and who buys it, the countries you want clients in, two secondary domains (about €20), and the person on your team who takes the handover.
Can you write on LinkedIn too?
Yes, from your own account if you want it, at a pace LinkedIn accepts.
What happens after 30 days?
You get the numbers: sent, replies, first calls held and qualified leads handed over. You decide whether to continue.
How is this different from buying a lead list?
A list or a data tool gives you names, or companies hiring a stack. We pick the ones that can actually buy what you sell, with the evidence, write to them in your name, and run the first call. Most of what you pay is for qualified leads.

Who it is for

Software companies with 5 to 100 people where the founder still sells.
