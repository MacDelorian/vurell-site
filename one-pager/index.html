Who to sell to, what to say to them, and the sending. Fixed scope, a public price, and the first draft before you commit.

Founders who shipped something good and have never sold anything. Selling was somebody else's job at every company you worked at before this one. One market at a time, chosen before anything is written.

Our method, in four steps

01Market.

We choose the first market and who in it can sign. One segment, named, and the people who cannot sign are named too.

02Message.

What to say, taken from who has the problem rather than from what the product does. Every line carries the quote it rests on.

03Sending.

The first twenty conversations. One note per name, carrying its source quote. You press Send, from your own account.

04First customers.

The pilot offer, and the replies worked together. Every no is written down with the reason it carried.

How an engagement runs

Week one.

You send five things. We read forty to fifty people in your segment and twenty names come back, each with the evidence behind it. You see the first draft before committing to anything.

Weeks two to five.

A note per name. You press Send. We work the replies with you up to the pilot ask.

After that.

A pilot where a reply carries one, and a written reason for every no.

What you receive

A private repository you own: the list, the evidence, the kill list, every message, every reply, the rules. Plain text, readable without us.

What we do not promise

No meetings are guaranteed. We forecast no reply rate, no number of customers and no revenue, here or anywhere else. Nothing goes out of your account without you pressing Send. Fixed scope and a public price at every rung, listed on vurell.com.
