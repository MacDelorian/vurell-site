Pricing

How much does Vurell cost?

There are three options, and every price is public. The pilot is €100 a month for the first two months, then €300 a month, plus €300 for each qualified call we hand you. There is no minimum term. The Signal & Positioning Sprint is €350 once and is credited in full to the pilot if it starts within 30 days. Sales on retainer is €500 a month, minimum 3 months, plus a fee on what your customers pay. The sample list before any of them is free.

Can I see a sample before I pay?

Yes. We send a free sample list: companies showing the signal for what you sell, each with the evidence and a link. No call needed. Ask for one.

Which option should I start with?

If you want to see the work before anything is sent, start with the sprint. If you want first calls with companies that need what you sell, start with the pilot. If you sell in the UK, the US or Ukraine, a deal is worth about €5k or more, and you want the deal taken to signature, ask about the retainer.

What is the Signal & Positioning Sprint?

Ten working days for €350: one page on how to position what you sell, the signal that says a company needs it, 50 companies showing it now, and the messages to write to them. The sprint sends nothing. You keep all of it, and the €350 comes off the pilot if the pilot starts within 30 days. What is in it.

How does sales on retainer work?

We act as your salesperson from first contact to signature: the list, emails, LinkedIn and phone, the demo, follow-ups and the proposal, inside a price range you set in writing. It costs €500 a month, minimum 3 months, then month to month with 14 days' notice. On top, a fee on what the customer actually pays: 15% of first-year contract value under €20k, 10% from €20k, or, for monthly SaaS, the customer's whole first monthly payment instead of the percentage. Details.

Is there a long contract?

No. The pilot runs month to month and you can stop at the end of any month. The retainer has a 3-month minimum, then runs month to month with 14 days' notice.

What if no qualified calls come?

Then you pay the monthly fee and nothing more: €100 in each of the first two months. You keep the company list, the messages and every reply.

How the pilot works

How soon do calls start?

The first days go to the signal, the list and the messages. Sending starts in the first week from domains that are already warmed up, and the first calls follow the first replies.

What do I need to provide?

What you sell and who buys it, the countries you want clients in, the list of companies already in your CRM, and the person on your team who takes the handover.

Who sends the emails?

We do, in your name, from our own warmed-up domains, so sending starts in the first week. If you want a sending domain of your own, we buy one that matches your brand, set up the inboxes and start the warm-up the same day: from €40, paid with the first month. Warm-up takes about two weeks, and until then we send from our domains. The domain stays yours.

Do I see the messages before they go out?

Yes. You read every message before the first send.

Can you write on LinkedIn too?

Yes, from your own account if you want it, at a pace LinkedIn accepts.

Who runs the calls?

We run the first call in English, French, Ukrainian or Russian, and hand the ones that are ready to your team with notes. Your team runs the sales call and sells. We never promise that a company will buy. If your buyers need another language, we tell you before the pilot starts.

Can you handle our inbound leads too?

Yes. Forward the form submissions to us. We answer each one within one working hour (09:00 to 19:00 CET, Monday to Friday), run the first call the same day, and check it against the criteria we agreed in writing. A qualified inbound call costs €150; the rest cost nothing.

What happens after the first month?

You get the numbers: sent, replies, first calls held and qualified calls handed over. Then you choose: carry on, or stop at the end of the month. The second month is €100 too; from the third the fee is €300 a month, always plus €300 per qualified call. If you want us to take the deals to signature, the next step is the sales retainer.

Leads and billing

What counts as a qualified call?

A company that fits the criteria we agree in writing before the pilot starts (the company size, the country, the role of the person), where we have held the first call with a decision-maker and handed it to your team with notes: the need, the timing and who decides. A call that does not happen, or a company that turns out not to fit, is not billed.

What is not billed?

A company that is already in your CRM or pipeline (send us the list before we start), a current client, a competitor, a duplicate, a company outside the criteria we agreed in writing, a person who is not in the agreed role, and any call that did not happen.

What if I disagree with a call?

Flag it within 5 working days of the handover and tell us why. We check it against the call notes and the agreed criteria and answer within 5 working days. A disputed call is not invoiced until it is settled. A call that moves once still counts when it happens.

How is this different from a meeting-booking agency?

A booking agency sells a number of meetings. We start from one public signal picked for what you sell and show you the companies that carry it before you pay. Then we write to them in your name, follow up by phone and run the first call ourselves. You pay €300 only for a call that fits the criteria we agree in writing, and the monthly fee is €100 for the first two months.

How is this different from buying a lead list or a data tool?

A list or a data tool gives you names, or companies hiring a stack. We pick the ones that can actually buy what you sell, with the evidence, write to them in your name, and run the first call. Most of what you pay is for qualified calls.

Markets, signals and email rules

Who is Vurell for?

Software companies with 5 to 100 people where the founder still sells: development studios, SaaS and custom software, from any country.

Which markets do you cover?

For the pilot, companies anywhere in Europe, found through public company registers, job boards and funding news in each country. We pick the countries with you before the pilot starts. The sales retainer is for companies selling in the UK, the US or Ukraine.

What is a buying signal?

A public sign that a company needs what you sell now. For a development studio it can be a developer role open for 30 days or more; for software for growing teams, a funding round that just closed; for sales tools, a company hiring salespeople. We pick the signal with you before the pilot starts. The signals we track.

Where do the companies on the list come from?

Public sources: company registers, job boards and funding news. Every company on the list shows the signal, with the evidence and a link. We do not sell lists by the thousand.

Is cold email to businesses allowed in Europe?

It depends on the country. In the UK and France, email to a company address about the person's work is allowed when the sender is named and there is a clear way to opt out. Germany, Austria, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Denmark and Poland are stricter, so we do not send cold email to companies there and write on LinkedIn instead. Every email we send says who we are and how to stop. This is how we work, not legal advice.

How do I stop messages from Vurell?

Reply stop to any message from us and we delete your record.
