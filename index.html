Questions.

What counts as a call?
A call that took place with a company that fits the criteria we agree in writing before the pilot starts: the company size, the country, and the role of the person on the call. A call that is booked and does not happen is not billed.
How much does it cost?
$500 up front for a 30-day pilot, then $200 for each held call. The sample list before it is free.
Which markets do you cover?
Germany and France first, through their public company registers and job boards. More of Europe next.
Who sends the emails?
We do, in your name, from two secondary domains you own. They cost about €20. You hold every account and every password.
Who runs the calls?
You do. We find the companies, write to them and book the calls. You sell. We never promise that a company will buy.
What if no calls happen?
Then you pay the $500 and nothing more. You keep the company list, the messages and every reply.
Who is it for?
Software companies with 5 to 100 people where the founder still sells: development studios, SaaS and custom software, from any country.
