Questions.

What counts as a qualified lead?
A company that fits the criteria we agree in writing before the pilot starts (the company size, the country, the role of the person), where we have held the first call with a decision-maker and handed it to your team with notes: the need, the timing and who decides. A call that does not happen, or a company that turns out not to fit, is not billed.
How much does it cost?
$500 up front for a 30-day pilot, then $300 for each qualified lead we hand you. The first 3 clients pay $0 up front, in exchange for a written review; 3 of 3 places are left. The sample list before it is free.
Which markets do you cover?
Germany and France first, through their public company registers and job boards. More of Europe next.
Who sends the emails?
We do, in your name, from two secondary domains you own. They cost about €20. You hold every account and every password.
Who runs the calls?
We run the first call, in English, French or Ukrainian, and hand the ones that are ready to your team with notes. Your team runs the sales call and sells. We never promise that a company will buy.
Can't I get this list from a data tool?
A data tool gives you companies that are hiring a stack. We pick the ones that can actually buy what you build, with the evidence, write to them in your name and run the first call. Most of what you pay is for qualified leads.
What if no qualified leads come?
Then you pay the $500 and nothing more. You keep the company list, the messages and every reply.
Who is it for?
Software companies with 5 to 100 people where the founder still sells: development studios, SaaS and custom software, from any country.
