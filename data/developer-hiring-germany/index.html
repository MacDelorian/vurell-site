German developer roles open 30 days or more: October 2026

On 16 October 2026 we read every software developer posting on the German Federal Employment Agency's job board for 8 stacks. Of 1,675 postings by small and mid-sized employers, 1,163 (69%) had been listed for 30 days or more.

What it means if you sell software development into Germany

A company that has been trying to hire a developer for two months is short of people now. Its job ad is public, dated and specific: the stack, the city, the team.

That is the signal Vurell uses. For a software studio or outsourcer, we find the companies hiring your stack, write to them in your name, and book the calls. $500 for a 30-day pilot, then $300 for each call that actually happens.

Method

Source. Bundesagentur für Arbeit, Jobsuche API (rest.arbeitsagentur.de, jobsuche-service v6). Read on 16 October 2026, every result page for each of React, Angular, Node.js, Java, Python, .NET, PHP and Flutter.

What counts. Regular jobs only: temporary work and private placement are switched off in the search. The title must name a software role. 75 postings by staffing and recruiting firms and 252 by public bodies and large groups are left out, because this snapshot is about small and mid-sized employers. Postings are counted once by their reference number.

Days listed. Counted from the first publication date. A repost under a new reference restarts that date, so every age is a floor. A posting stays on the board until the employer removes it, so some long-listed ads are evergreen rather than unfilled.

Data. report.json and report.csv hold every figure on this page. Employer names are not published. Licence: CC BY 4.0. Updated monthly.

Questions

How many developer jobs in Germany stay open for more than 30 days?
On 16 October 2026, 1,163 of 1,675 software developer postings by small and mid-sized employers on the German Federal Employment Agency's job board (69%) had been listed for 30 days or more. 41% had been listed for 90 days or more. The median posting had been listed for 62 days.
Which programming stacks are hardest to hire for in Germany?
Among stacks with at least 100 postings on 16 October 2026, PHP has both the highest share of roles listed for 30 days or more (79%) and the longest median listing (106 days). Full figures for React, Angular, Node.js, Java, Python, .NET, PHP and Flutter are in the table on this page.
Where in Germany are developer roles open the longest?
By count of roles listed for 30 days or more, the states with the most were Bavaria (263), North Rhine-Westphalia (219), Baden-Württemberg (183). The cities with the most were Hamburg (56), Berlin (54), München (50).
Does a job posting open for 90 days mean the role is unfilled?
Not always. A posting stays on the board until the employer takes it down, so some long-listed ads are evergreen. A repost under a new reference restarts the date, so the ages here are a floor for how long a role has been listed.
What does this mean for software companies selling into Germany?
A company that has been trying to hire a developer for two months or more is short of people now. That is a public signal a software studio or outsourcer can act on. Vurell finds those companies for your stack and books the calls.
