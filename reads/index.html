Landing page read

Covera

covera-agents.com. AI agents for insurance brokers. Read 7 September 2026.

What the page says it sells

"AI agents for insurance brokers." Under it, "Less admin, more selling."

What survives

"It enters the risk profile into each carrier portal to request quotes, mapping fields from one carrier to the next. No more double entry." An agency owner knows what that costs, because they have done it. It names a task, in their words, with an end state.

Six named pains in the buyer's own language. "Missing documents." "Re-keying across carrier portals." "Late follow-ups." Listed flat, no adjectives.

The integrations. "AMS360, EZLynx, HawkSoft, Applied Epic" tells a broker at a glance whether this is for them.

What does not

"12× ROI after 4 weeks." Twelve times what. No price, no denominator, no method.

"We got back around ten hours a week from servicing and data entry." Signed "Commercial lines brokerage, San José". Brokers buy from brokers they can call. An unnamed agency cannot be called.

"17 hrs saved · 12× ROI on quoting · 320 files handled." These sit inside the dashboard mock, in the same visual weight as live product state.

Who I would write to first

"Insurance brokers and captive agents, from local agencies to larger, established firms." The page names them once, in the FAQ, near the bottom. Put it first. Then carrier re-keying, alone, because it is the sharpest thing here.

Landing page read

Forward

useforward.co. Private credit origination. Read 7 September 2026.

What the page says it sells

"Origination Made Easy." Then, "Borrower inputs become fund ready outputs in minutes."

What survives

"Funds receive mandate matched deals with risks and open diligence items alongside source evidence." Screening is the job. The cost of the job is finding out late what could have been known first. It is the one thing on the page a reader can picture arriving.

The worked example. "Acme, Inc. | $10M Facility | Senior Secured", then "Tenor: 36 months", "Pricing: 10.5% p.a.", "Covenants: Net leverage ≤ 3.50x". It shows the output instead of describing it, and a credit reader can judge in one look whether the structure is sane.

What does not

"$20B AUM of funds using Forward to originate deals." AUM belongs to the fund. It says nothing about how many deals moved through the product.

"25+ Hours saved per analyst, weekly." No base, no sample, no period. An analyst's week is finite and this is a large slice of it.

"Origination Made Easy." In private credit, origination means sourcing. This page does not source deals. It structures materials for deals already in hand.

Who I would write to first

The fund. The page reads to two readers and never chooses. "so your team can focus on underwriting and closing" is a credit fund. "We handle the packaging, you focus on getting deals funded" is whoever packages the borrower. Choose the fund, and open on screening.
