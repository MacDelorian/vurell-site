The free count

Who could sign

Paste your last 20 recipients, one per line: title, company, and a note if you have one. You get the number who could sign without asking anybody, and a verdict on every line.

It runs inside this page. Nothing you paste leaves your browser: no server, no request, nothing stored.

What sign means

A signer is a person who can commit budget and put their name on an agreement without asking anyone above them.

Can sign and studio count as signers. Cannot sign alone and needs a read do not.

Why the signer count is the number to watch

The list decides more than the message does. Twenty names are twenty choices about who reads you, and every one of them is made before the first word is written.

A search for who has the problem returns the person who does the work. That person can want the thing and still not be able to buy it, and a list built that way fills with people who cannot sign.

A signer reads your message as a decision: yes, no, or not now. A non-signer reads it as something to forward, and a forward is a second message you did not write, to a reader you did not choose.

So count before you write. When the signers on a list are few, rewriting the message changes nothing about who reads it.

What this does not know

This is a title read, not a person read. It reads the words you pasted and nothing else. It does not open a profile, it does not know who holds the budget, and a title is wrong often enough that the number here is a floor, not a fact.

The paid rung reads the person: the profile, the company page and the last things they wrote, under written gates, with the quoted line that decided it.

The rules, in the order they run

The first rule that fires decides the row. Rules 8 to 10 run after the seven, on the optional fields only: rules 8 and 9 can move a row, rule 10 adds a confidence.

  1. A line with nothing on it that reads as a title is cannot tell, needs a read. Nothing is guessed.
    linkedin.com/in/someone-i-met-at-a-meetup
  2. If any line in your list carries a sales or business-development title at the same company, every line at that company is cannot sign alone, including the sales line itself. Somebody there already owns the sale.
    VP Business Development, Harrow & Pike (18 people) moves CEO, Harrow & Pike (18 people)
  3. An owner title signs: Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, Owner, Managing Director, Managing Partner, Geschäftsführer, Gründer, Oprichter, President, Partner, Principal. If the company reads as an agency, consultancy or studio, the verdict is studio: they can still sign, they just sell services to other people too.
    Oprichter, Stroomlijn BV signs. Owner, Two Kettles Consultancy is studio.
  4. Cannot sign alone: marketing, growth, content, community, design and engineering titles, and Manager, Coordinator, Specialist, Analyst, Intern, Student, Assistant, Associate, Trainee.
    Marketing Manager, Ashgrove Dairy
  5. VP or Head at a company over about 200 people, when the line gives a headcount: cannot sign alone. The signature is upstairs.
    Head of Product, Sable Freight (900 employees)
  6. Head of a firm under about 50 people, when the line gives a headcount: can sign. At that size the head is the company. Director at an agency, consultancy or studio: studio.
    Head of Operations, Kestrel Tooling (22 people) signs. Director, Pale Fox Studio is studio.
  7. Anything left over is cannot tell, needs a read.
    Director, Halyard Freight
  8. A company type word this page cannot score: marketplace, nonprofit, university, public sector. The row is cannot tell, needs a read, whatever the seven rules said.
    Founder, Harbour Lane Marketplace is a read. Owner, Riverside Nonprofit is a read.
  9. A C-level title other than CEO: CTO, CFO, COO, CMO, or any Chief Something Officer. With a headcount under 50 on the line: can sign. Otherwise: cannot sign alone. This runs only on rows the seven left on rule 4 or rule 7. A row an earlier rule decided keeps that rule.
    CTO, Kelpline Systems (30 people) signs. CFO, Sable Freight (900 employees) is cannot sign alone. COO, Ashgrove Dairy is cannot sign alone.
  10. Every row gets a confidence: high when the line carries a title and a headcount, medium with a title only, low whenever the verdict is cannot tell, needs a read, whatever else the line carries.
    Head of Operations, Kestrel Tooling (22 people) is high. Founder, Northbeam Instruments is medium. Director, Halyard Freight is low.
