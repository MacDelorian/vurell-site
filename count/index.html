    The rules, in the order they run

    1. A line with nothing on it that reads as a title is cannot tell, needs a read. Nothing is guessed.
    2. If any line in your list carries a sales or business-development title at the same company, every line at that company is cannot sign alone — including the sales line itself. Somebody there already owns the sale.
    3. An owner title signs: Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, Owner, Managing Director, Managing Partner, Geschäftsführer, Gründer, Oprichter, President, Partner, Principal. If the company reads as an agency, consultancy or studio, the verdict is studio — they can still sign, they just sell services to other people too.
    4. Cannot sign alone: marketing, growth, content, community, design and engineering titles, and Manager, Coordinator, Specialist, Analyst, Intern, Student, Assistant, Associate, Trainee.
    5. VP or Head at a company over about 200 people, when the line gives a headcount: cannot sign alone. The signature is upstairs.
    6. Head of a firm under about 50 people, when the line gives a headcount: can sign — at that size the head is the company. Director at an agency, consultancy or studio: studio.
    7. Anything left over is cannot tell, needs a read.

    What this is not

    This is a title read, not a person read. It reads the words you pasted and nothing else. It does not open a profile, it does not know who holds the budget, and a title is wrong often enough that the number here is a floor, not a fact.

    The paid rung reads the person: the profile, the company page and the last things they wrote, under written gates, with the quoted line that decided it.
